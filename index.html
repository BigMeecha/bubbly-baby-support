Bubbly Baby Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The short version

Bubbly Baby does not collect, sell or share your data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What the app stores

The entries you log (feeds, sleep, diapers, tummy time, wake-ups), your baby's name and birthday, and the name you choose for yourself. This is stored on your device and synced through Apple's iCloud to your own iCloud account, so it is protected by your Apple ID. The developer cannot see it.

Sharing with a partner

If you invite someone, Apple's iCloud sharing makes your baby's entries available to the people you invite, and only to them. You can remove a person or stop sharing at any time from the app's share button.

Notifications

Reminders and partner notifications are created on your device. No notification content is sent to the developer.

Exports

When you export to CSV or JSON, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Links to Apple Music or Spotify open those apps; no data is sent with them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove synced data, delete it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your device.

Children

Bubbly Baby is for parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions or support: open an issue on GitHub.
